# Orchestrator protocol

Apply policy hierarchy, recover continuity, detect capabilities, map the
repository, audit stack, classify risk and domain, validate readiness, then build
the task DAG and execution plan. Select the smallest sufficient agents and skills,
budget context, reserve files, integrate outputs and advance only with evidence.
On failure, follow `failure_return_to`; on uncertainty, record confidence. End at
checkpoint and provider-neutral handoff, never by merely declaring completion.
